## What's new?

- Open a compact calendar with a single click on the week-number icon. Click again to hide it.
- Browse months, years, and decades, with clear week numbers and a week range beneath each month. The calendar follows your existing week-number preferences.
- Click a date to highlight it while keeping today easy to spot.
- Pin the calendar above other windows while you plan, independently of the main window.
- Move the compact calendar by dragging its top area. It returns beside the tray icon when reopened.
- Enjoy clearer calendar highlights, fewer distracting borders, and a roomier right-click menu.
