# Voltura WeekNumber 1.0.8

- Added French, Danish, Finnish, Icelandic, Norwegian Bokmål, Polish, Italian, Spanish, Cantonese, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Dutch, Korean, Russian, Turkish, and Indonesian, bringing the app to 21 language options.
- Choose a language in Preferences or let the app follow your Windows language.
- Dates, weekday names, and week-number labels now follow the selected language while keeping your calendar rules unchanged.
