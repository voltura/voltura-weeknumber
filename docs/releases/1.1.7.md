## What's new?

- **Import calendars** from **.ics files** or **web links** to see holidays, appointments, and other events in the tray calendar, including recurring events.
- Spot days with events by a small dot, then click a date to see event times, locations, descriptions, and the calendar they came from.
- Manage your imported calendars in one place. Add calendars, replace an import with an updated file, or remove a calendar and its events.
